Radioactive Remediation☢️
Central Asia2019—2023

Uranium tailings remediation

Challenge

A uranium-mining legacy tailings facility posed a long-term risk of radioactive contamination.

Approach

Isolation and stabilization of the facility, relocation and safe disposal of radioactive material, multi-layer protective capping.

Result

1.2M m³ of material remediated, the site brought into IAEA compliance, with long-term radiological monitoring in place.
