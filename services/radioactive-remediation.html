Radioactive Remediation

Elimination of radioactive contamination on land and at uranium mining tailings facilities and nuclear legacy sites. Decontamination, isolation, long-term stabilization and monitoring in compliance with IAEA requirements.

Our Approach

Track record: 2M+ m³ of radioactive material remediated and 20+ tailings facilities brought to a safe, stable condition — independently verified by our in-house radiological laboratory.

  • Radiological survey and contamination assessment
  • Decontamination and containment design in line with IAEA requirements
  • Relocation, safe disposal and protective capping
  • Long-term radiological monitoring by our in-house laboratory
