Health, Safety & Environment

Safety is not optional — it's the standard

Working with radioactive and chemically hazardous sites, ERN places the safety of personnel, local communities and the environment above schedule and cost. Our HSE policy applies across every ERN site.

🦺
Occupational Safety

Personnel training, PPE, work-permit procedures for radiological and chemically hazardous sites, compliance with ISO 45001 / OHSAS 18001.

🌍
Environmental Management

ISO 14001 environmental management system: minimizing impact on soil, water and air at every project stage.

🤝
Community Engagement

Public communication and health protection for communities near project sites, engagement throughout delivery.

🔬
Radiological Control

Our in-house radiological laboratory provides independent dosimetric control on every site — the same laboratory behind more than 2 million m³ of radioactive material remediated to date.
