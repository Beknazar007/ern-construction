About the Company

More than remediation.
Full engineering lifecycle.

ERN Construction is a multi-discipline engineering and construction company. We specialize in complex projects that demand deep expertise, experience, and accountability.

To date, ERN has remediated more than 2 million m³ of radioactive material and brought 20+ tailings facilities to a safe, stable condition, independently verified by our in-house radiological laboratory on every site.

We take on challenges others decline: radioactive contamination elimination, sites with hazardous chemicals (HSMP), municipal solid waste landfill closure and remediation, construction of hydraulic structures — tailings facilities, HPPs, dams.

We also deliver transport infrastructure, commercial and industrial buildings — held to the same standards of quality and environmental responsibility.

Green Economy

Contamination is
an untapped resource

ERN views every problem site as an opportunity. A reclaimed landfill, a closed tailings facility, a remediated industrial plot — these are land, energy, and infrastructure returned to the green economy.

♻️
Landfills → Territory

Reclaimed dumps become green zones, solar energy sites, or construction land. Extracted biogas provides a source of renewable energy.

💧
Waterways → Clean Energy

Small HPPs on natural waterways deliver stable, zero-emission power. ERN's hydraulic expertise enables delivery in the most challenging terrain.

🌱
Contamination → Recovery

Every reclaimed hectare means restored biodiversity, clean water, and a safe living environment. Long-term monitoring guarantees lasting results.

E — ENVIRONMENT

Reducing environmental damage, restoring biodiversity, minimizing carbon footprint across all sites

S — SOCIAL

Worker safety, community engagement, and public health protection near project sites

G — GOVERNANCE

Transparency, IAEA and World Bank compliance, international audit, long-term accountability for every site
